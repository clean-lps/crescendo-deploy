Discuss my vehicle
Does it replace PPF?

They serve different purposes. Coating is a surface treatment; PPF adds a film. Share your priorities to assess the right option.

Does it remove existing scratches?

The treatment alone does not correct damage. Any additional paint preparation should be assessed and included in the proposal.

Do I still need to wash my vehicle?

Yes. Maintenance is still necessary and should follow the applied product’s requirements. Ask for washing and inspection guidance.
