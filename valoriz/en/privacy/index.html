Information about how this website works.

Your quote request

The form sends the service, vehicle details, your name, email, optional phone number and the details you include to the Valoriz team so they can respond to your inquiry. Submission uses the website’s email service.

Information during browsing

The fields remain on the page while you complete your request and are not stored in a website profile. This site does not use advertising cookies. It may temporarily keep campaign tags from the URL in your browser session to include the inquiry source in the message or booking link.

Email and external services

The email service processes your details to deliver your request to Valoriz. The booking calendar, Instagram and Google Maps are external services with their own terms and privacy practices. The Google map loads when you open the Contact page.

Questions about your data

For questions about how your inquiry is handled or to request a correction, email clara@valorizcorp.com.
